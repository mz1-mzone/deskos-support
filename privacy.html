DeskOS Privacy Policy

Updated October 15, 2026

Your workspace and your data

DeskOS has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. The developer does not operate a server that receives your workspace files, browsing activity, or AI conversations.

Data stored on your device

Workspace settings, notes, projects, imported documents, browser history, website data, and cookies are stored on your device. You can delete content in DeskOS and clear website data in Settings. Removing the app removes its local app data; files you exported elsewhere remain in those locations.

Optional AI assistant

Starting with version 1.1.1, you can connect your own OpenAI or Anthropic API account. This is optional: the desktop and creative tools work without an AI connection. Before connecting, DeskOS asks for your permission to share the information needed by the assistant.

When you run the assistant, your request, that provider's conversation history and summaries, selected app text, file names, and project structure may be sent directly to the provider you selected. Browser assistance may include the current page address and visible page content, including email or message content you ask it to work with. These requests are associated with your provider account. The App Store privacy label therefore discloses Other User Content, Emails or Text Messages, and Browsing History for App Functionality, linked to you and not used for tracking.

DeskOS does not automatically send your photo or video files, cookies, passwords, or existing form-field values to the AI provider. Your API key is stored in the device Keychain and is sent only to its provider to authorize requests. API usage is billed by the provider under your account; consumer chat subscriptions do not include API usage.

Session conversations and compacted summaries are held in memory until you clear the chat or close DeskOS. Disconnecting a provider removes its saved key and clears that provider's session conversation. Providers may retain requests under their own policies and your account settings; clearing a local chat does not delete a provider's retained copy. See OpenAI's privacy policy and Anthropic's privacy policy, and use your provider's privacy controls for deletion requests.

The assistant can fill browser fields and prepare email drafts when you request it. Websites may automatically save those drafts. Sending an email requires your final review in DeskOS.

Files, photos, and recordings

DeskOS accesses its own documents and files or folders you select through the system file picker. Photo-library access follows the permission you grant, including limited access. Media editing and rendering run on your device. DeskOS does not bypass system protections or read other apps' private files. You choose when to export, share, print, or upload content. Text you choose to use with the optional AI assistant is handled as described above.

Websites and third-party services

The browser and web shortcuts connect directly to websites you choose to visit. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. Website logins and cookies can remain on your device until you sign out or clear them. DeskOS does not receive data you submit directly to a website.

Children's privacy

DeskOS does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The built-in browser provides unrestricted access to the web, so a parent or guardian should supervise a child's use.

Changes

This policy will be updated if DeskOS data practices change. The current effective date appears at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a support request.
